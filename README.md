# Frame Player with Android Remote

This fork of [Frame Player](https://github.com/risenxxx/frame-player) adds an Android remote for the Windows player.

Pair your phone once over the local network to control playback, manage the queue, toggle repeat, and switch audio or subtitle tracks. Use the jog wheel to seek through the video and follow your position with a live preview on your phone.

While casting to a TV, choose **TV stereo (2.0)** under **Audio output** in the Android remote to downmix surround tracks such as 5.1 and 7.1 to stereo.
